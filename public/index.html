    Track Record — did the picks actually work?

    Every day, this app logs its own "Today's Best" / "Momentum" / "Reversal" picks with the price at that moment, then quietly updates each pick's latest price every day after — so you can look back honestly at whether a pick from a week or a month ago actually worked out, instead of only ever seeing today's opinion.

    Weekend Picks — best 5–8 stocks for Monday

    Every weekend, while the market's closed, this scans the FULL Nifty 50 + Midcap + Smallcap universe once and ranks everything by its own historical odds: high probability of profit, negligible probability of loss — based on how this exact stock has actually behaved after similar signals in the past, not a guess. The best handful get frozen here until next weekend.

    Model Learning — is your own track record actually being learned from?

    Every technical signal this app uses (Golden trend, RSI zone, volume spike, MACD, etc.) gets its own real win-rate here, Regime-aware learning from resolved picks (target/SL). NORMAL vs CRISIS separated so crash days do not poison normal stats. Shows Overall / Regime / Expectancy / sample / confidence / why. Small capped nudge only (±0.5) — never overrides Technical + R:R + Market. Not a future profit probability.
